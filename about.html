OUR STORY

Vision

To provide care and support for orphans, vulnerable children, less-privileged children, women, and widows; to empower children with quality education so they can have a better life and a good future.

Mission

Embrace orphans, vulnerable children, less-privileged children, women, and widows with the love of God, providing for their needs.

How We Started

To the glory of God, we have been giving money, food, and materials to individuals to meet their needs at one capacity or the other. At some point, we were inspired to establish the good works as a Charity Foundation under the umbrella of the Hope for the Hopeless Mission of Christ Heritage Tabernacle Ministries, also known as Christ Heritage Church.

The Charity Foundation was registered under the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) as J-Care for the Hopeless Charity Foundation, also known as J-Care Charity Foundation.

Our Journey

30th June, 2020

Inauguration

The J-Care Charity Foundation was officially inaugurated.

20th December, 2020

First Outreach

We reached out to 52 children from 30 families. Activities included enrollment, prayers, health talk and counselling, distribution of scholastic materials (school bags, textbooks, notebooks), welfare provision (clothes, shoes, kitchen items), and feeding.

4th April, 2021

Second Outreach

Continued our mission to support the vulnerable in our community.

24th December, 2021

Third Outreach

Expanded our reach to support 120 children.

December 2025

Present Impact

Since April 2022, the numbers of the children have been increasing, and presently, we take care of 430 children.
