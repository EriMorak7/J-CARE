WHAT WE DO

We support communities through 7 core pillars, turning lived experience into lasting change.

1. Educational Support

Empowering children with quality education so they can have a better life and a good future.

  • Payment of Children School Fees: to increase opportunities for children to access education, improve learning, make stable and focused.
  • Provision of Scholastic materials: Textbooks, exercise books, pens, pencils, erasers, sharpeners, rulers, school uniforms, school bags, and school sandals.
  • Conduct School Outreaches: to check up on children's current educational status and devise means to support those in dire needs.
  • Extramural programs: for children in terminal classes.

2. Feeding

Providing essential nutrition to those who need it most.

  • Provision of raw food items: like rice, beans, garri, spaghetti, indomie, drinks, water, and other kinds of food items for children.

3. Welfare

Ensuring basic needs are met for a life of dignity.

  • Provision of clothes, shoes, and other useful materials: for children and widows, giving the children, especially, a sense of belonging amongst their peers.

4. Healthcare for All

Providing medical access and health education to vulnerable populations.

5. Socialise & Interact

Building community, joy, and spiritual grounding.

  • Organise Carnivals & Excursions: for children's relaxation.
  • Christmas Party.
  • Church Programs: to develop children to have faith in God and build trust in Him for a better and very brighter future.

6. Skills Acquisition

Practical empowerment for long-term success.

  • Organise DIY training programs: for children to develop their skills in useful items for school and on the long run improve their educational status.

7. Women and Widows

Supporting mothers and widows to build sustainable livelihoods.

  • Skills acquisition: e.g., Soap making, gele tying, etc.
  • Small-scale business support.
  • Transport fare supports.
  • Organises programs for prayers and counselling: to stabilise their emotions.
