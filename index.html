WHAT WE DO

We support communities through 7 core pillars — from education and feeding to women empowerment and spiritual development.

Educational Support

Payment of school fees, scholastic materials, and extramural programs.

Feeding & Welfare

Provision of raw food items, clothes, and shoes for children and widows.

Women & Widows

Skills acquisition (soap making, gele tying) and business support.
